For Respond & Acclaim add | psalm | 2026-11-08 or | acclamation | 2026-11-08. Catalog aliases may follow as | BB=517,MI=517,ACCOMP=216. Page offset = PDF page number minus printed page number (0 if they match). Name Mass settings like the outline, e.g. "Mass of Saint Ann".
