Weekly Mass slides guide

Reference for building each week's Mass PowerPoint, like the bulletin style guide. The complete reusable prompt is here: Download the full master prompt (.txt)

Weekly steps

  1. Download the week's readings from the USCCB readings calendar (use the Sunday entry) and the weekly music (the choir's musing).
  2. Start from the weekly PowerPoint, with the standard slides included in every week's set.
  3. Give the PowerPoint, the master prompt and the approved design examples (Entrance Song, reading, musical notation, Profession of Faith, announcements) to the AI tool.
  4. Ask for Stages 1–3 only (inventory, seasonal theme, previews). Review and approve the previews.
  5. Authorize the full build (Stage 5), then check it (Stage 6).

The six stages

Approval process
StageWhat happens
1. InventoryList every section with start and end slide (blank black slides mark boundaries), content type, design approach, and whether extra slides are needed.
2. Seasonal themeConfirm Mass date, liturgical season and celebration; propose colors and imagery. Ask if unsure.
3. PreviewsActual images of the first slide of every major section (opening, songs, readings, notation, prayers, announcements).
4. ApprovalStop and wait. Nothing is built until the designs are approved.
5. BuildApply approved designs in original Mass order, adding slides as needed.
6. Quality controlCheck completeness, order, clipping, contrast, alignment, and that the file opens in PowerPoint.

Design by section

Each section has its own look within one design system
SectionDesign
Songs (Entrance, Psalm, Gospel Acclamation, Gifts, Communion, Going Forth)Entrance Song is the master: decorative title banner, large bold centered lyrics, photo background, copyright line. Each song gets its own background.
Readings (First, Second, Gospel)Clear title area, scripture reference, uncluttered text panel, aligned header and body. Title only on the first slide; split at natural breaks.
Musical notationNotation as large as practical, never altered or redrawn, subtle background, lyrics kept with notes, split at musical breaks.
Profession of Faith and prayersText-focused with strong contrast. PROFESSION OF FAITH heading on the first slide only.
AnnouncementsWarm background, large bold white centered text, no song banners. Content comes only from the current week.
Opening slideSeasonal imagery and the celebration name.

Seasonal colors

  • Advent: violet, deep blue, candlelight, subtle gold.
  • Christmas: gold, ivory, white, rich red.
  • Ordinary Time: ocean blues, greens, golden sunlight.
  • Lent: violet and muted earth tones. Holy Week: restrained and reverent.
  • Easter: ivory, white, luminous gold. Pentecost: red and gold.
  • All Saints / All Souls: autumn leaves, gold, burnt orange, burgundy, sunset.
  • Marian feasts and other solemnities: colors suited to the celebration.

Standard slides that change by season

Standard slideUsedLeft out
Alleluia (Gospel Acclamation)All seasons except LentLent (Ash Wednesday to the Easter Vigil): use the Lenten acclamation instead, e.g. "Praise to you, Lord Jesus Christ, King of endless glory" or "Glory and praise to you, Lord Jesus Christ". The Alleluia returns at the Easter Vigil.
GloriaOrdinary Time, Christmas, Easter, solemnities and feastsAdvent and Lent, except solemnities and feasts that fall in them (e.g. Immaculate Conception, St. Joseph, Annunciation)
CreedSundays and solemnitiesWeekdays
Holy Thursday to Easter VigilSpecial rules; AV Ministry will add these as special slides.

The weekly package should apply these automatically from the church calendar. Tell me about any other slides that change by season or parish custom.

Prelude and Going Forth: the music before and after Mass may be secular. They are optional uploads, and the season rules above do not apply to them. Keep any credit or license line supplied.

Special Masses

MassLook and slide changes
WeddingIvory and gold, couple's names on the opening slide. Gloria used; Creed optional. Special slides for vows blessing if supplied.
Funeral / MemorialQuiet white or violet, name and dates of the deceased. Gloria, Creed and Alleluia are normally left out (confirm with the pastor). Commendation slides as special slides.
BaptismWhite and water imagery. Add baptismal promises and the names of those baptized as special slides.
First CommunionWhite and gold, names of the children, simple large text.
ConfirmationRed and gold (the Holy Spirit). Renewal of baptismal promises as special slides.
Holy Day of ObligationColor of the feast. Gloria and Creed used as for a Sunday, even on a weekday.

The seasonal rules above still apply unless the feast overrides them. Every special Mass starts from the same standard slides, with its own special slides added.

Florida Keys imagery

Sunsets, turquoise water, palms, mangroves, boats and golden reflections, blended with the season's colors. Backgrounds must never compete with the text.

Rules that always apply

  • Readability from the back of the church comes first. Never shrink text to fit; add slides instead.
  • The uploaded PowerPoint is the source of truth: keep all lyrics, readings, prayers, notation, announcements, dates and times. Do not invent, summarize or silently omit; flag unclear text.
  • Keep every copyright and music-licensing notice exactly as supplied.
  • 16:9, editable in PowerPoint, consistent margins and fonts, no needless animation.
  • Check every slide for clipped words, misalignment, poor contrast and wrong order.
