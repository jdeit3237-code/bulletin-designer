Mass slides

Upload the week's songs, readings and announcements. The standard slides are kept here and are always included. Then preview the slides and download the PowerPoint. See the Slides guide.

  1. Upload the standard slides once (they stay).
  2. Pick the Mass date, then upload the week's songs, readings, announcements and any special slides.
  3. Tick bulletin graphics you want, press Save ticked graphics.
  4. Press Preview slides to check every slide, then Download PowerPoint.
