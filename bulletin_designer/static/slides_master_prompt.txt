**Advent**
- Violet, deep blue, candlelight, and subtle gold.
- Imagery suggesting hope, preparation, and anticipation.

**Christmas**
- Warm gold, ivory, white, and rich red.
- Nativity imagery and the joy of Christ's birth.

**Ordinary Time**
- Ocean blues, natural greens, golden sunlight, and peaceful coastal landscapes.
- A fresh, welcoming, and reverent atmosphere.

**Lent**
- Violet, muted earth tones, and restrained imagery.
- A contemplative, prayerful appearance.

**Holy Week**
- Reverent, dramatic imagery appropriate to the Passion of Christ.
- A restrained design that supports reflection.

**Easter**
- Bright ivory, white, and luminous gold.
- Imagery suggesting resurrection, hope, and new life.

**Pentecost**
- Rich red, gold, and imagery representing the Holy Spirit.

**All Saints Day and All Souls Day**
- Autumn leaves, warm gold, burnt orange, burgundy, and sunset colors.
- Imagery reflecting the communion of saints, remembrance, and eternal life.

**Marian celebrations**
- Appropriate Marian colors and imagery that complement the parish's established visual style.

**Other feast days and solemnities**
- Select colors, imagery, and backgrounds that reflect the particular celebration.

These are creative guidelines rather than rigid templates.

If the date or liturgical celebration cannot be established confidently, ask me before selecting the final seasonal theme.
